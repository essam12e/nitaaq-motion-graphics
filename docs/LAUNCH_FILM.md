# Launch / Hype Film

Code: `tease-reveal` and `feature-montage` in `src/scenes/commerce/product-director.tsx`, genre arc `launch` (`src/director/plan.ts`), `launch-hype` style, transition engine rhythm rules, procedural soundtrack.

## Structure

`tease → reveal → montage → feature → product → offer → cta` (opener `tease` replaces the hook when there is material to withhold). Beats without material are dropped; a supplied screenshot or product is never lost (a `demo`/`product` beat is inserted).

- `tease-reveal` — slit (a thin light slit opens onto the reveal), countdown (3-2-1 in the project numerals), word-tease (tease lines, then the name). Tease copy comes from `content.tease` (split into clauses) or the hook; the reveal is `content.reveal`, the product name or the brand. `content.launchDate` is shown under the reveal.
- `feature-montage` — flash (one big word per beat) or rail (features stream past the product).
- Escalation: the AI Creative Director's energy curve builds to the hero (the reveal or the product), and the CTA resolves.

## Hype ≠ chaos

- Rhythmic hard cuts (≥ 25 % of cuts, more on beats), one flashy transition budget, never the same transition twice in a row.
- One hero moment; the soundtrack's downbeat/drop is placed on it (`heroSec`), the Sound Director puts a build (riser ending exactly on the reveal) and one impact there, and avoids doubling the soundtrack's own hits.
- Hierarchy stays: one message per scene, CTA readable and held.
- `launch-hype` style (near-black stage, one hot colour, hard light) is used when the wording is hype/bold («حماس»، «جريء»، "hype", teaser); otherwise the brand or industry style.
